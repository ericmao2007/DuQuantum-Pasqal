What this page does — and what the run means

Real data, replayed locally

Clustering attempts, rejection reasons, measured routing histograms, and intermediate atom excitation probabilities come from the Python emulator. Playback changes the displayed snapshot; it does not run a new solver in your browser.

Two different spaces

US cities are the delivery locations. The tiny atom register is a separate layout in micrometers that represents route-edge decisions. Its geometry is fitted to a QUBO and is not a miniature map of the United States.

An honest hybrid

Routing for up to three customers is marked classical/trivial. Four-customer routes use measured edge-encoding samples with emulator-only DMM controls. Larger clusters use a labeled classical size fallback. Full exact references are evaluation only.

Distance & uncertainty

Distances are straight-line equirectangular projections at 38° latitude, not driving directions. Shared finite shot banks and a small instance are not evidence of quantum advantage. Occupations are probabilities, not a definite vehicle route during evolution.
